# Narration recording script

The dialogue below matches the current game. Each heading is a cue key in src/narration.js. A full-cue MP3 should contain all its numbered passages in order. For separate speakers, record one file per numbered passage and map those files as an array in the same order. You can record only the scenes you want; the rest use browser voices. See [AUDIO.md](AUDIO.md) for setup and playback controls.

Suggested full-cue filenames match the headings, such as opening.mp3, revelation.mp3, and whisper-lower-1.mp3. Keep ordinary and immortal branches separate. If you change the wording, update the written game text to match before publishing.

## opening

1. **Narrator:** Your clan is gone. Your masters lie beneath the ashes.

2. **Narrator:** The Radiant Yang Sect took everything—and carried your ancestral heirloom to the summit.

3. **Narrator:** Tonight, you climb their mountain uninvited. Slip past their watchmen, or silence them. Reclaim what is yours.

## transition-lower

1. **Narrator:** The first pavilion falls behind you. These watchmen sounded young. Afraid. Your masters deserved better than hesitation. Above, another barrier hums against the night.

## transition-middle

1. **Narrator:** The second barrier shatters. Its inscriptions were turned toward the summit. The sacred heirloom calls more clearly now, its pulse disturbingly like your own.

## transition-upper

1. **Narrator:** The final prison layer breaks. You remember no massacre. No masters. Only centuries of sleep, and the part of your soul waiting at the summit.

## revelation

1. **Narrator:** The grandmaster kneels inside a broken Bagua formation. Blood runs from his eyes. He is holding the seal together with his life.

2. **Grandmaster:** “There was no clan. No stolen heirloom. You are the Yin Ghost General.”

3. **Narrator:** The thing calling to you is your severed soul-core. Each courtyard was a layer of your prison. You were never breaking in. You were breaking out.

4. **Narrator:** The grandmaster rises. Behind him, the battered sect still lives. He draws his sword.

## revelation-immortal

2. **Narrator:** There was no justice waiting at the summit. Only your own ancient power, finally whole.
